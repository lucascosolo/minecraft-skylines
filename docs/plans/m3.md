# Milestone 3 plan: Minecraft blocks and GUI in the city

**Done when (in game):** a block placed in Minecraft mode appears in the city at the right spot
with Minecraft's textures, is hidden behind buildings and terrain and hides them in turn, can be
stood on, and the Minecraft hotbar/inventory is visible and usable over the city view.

Contract: `protocol/minecraft-skylines-v1.md` (1.2 section). Ledger: `.agent/ledger-m3.md`.

## Design

- Minecraft (guest) meshes its blocks with its own block renderer and sends `SECTION_MESH` per 16³
  section plus `BLOCK_ATLAS` (PNG); CS1 (host) builds Unity meshes (z mirrored, winding reversed)
  and draws them on the Props layer with the game's prop shader, which the T1 test showed renders
  with correct depth against terrain (and through clipped terrain).
- Open: how the prop shader's maps (`_MainTex`, `_XYSMap`, `_ACIMap`, `_Color`) must be set to get
  Minecraft's atlas colours, alpha cutout and vertex tint; settled with an in-game material
  experiment driven by the self-test (several material variants side by side, screenshots).
- Collision for placed blocks already works on the Minecraft side (they are real blocks there).
- GUI: Minecraft renders HUD and screens; pixels reach CS1 through a shared-memory file and are
  drawn as a screen-space overlay (later chunk; transport decided after the block path works).
- `DEBUG_COMMAND` lets the CS1 self-test place test structures in the dev world (dev flag only).
