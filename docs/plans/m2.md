# Milestone 2 plan: walk the city with Minecraft physics

**Done when (in game):** in a test city, the player switches to Minecraft mode, sees the city
first-person from Minecraft's eye position, walks, sprints, jumps, climbs a road ramp, crosses a
bridge deck and walks under it, follows slopes without floating or sinking, cannot walk up cliffs,
and presses Esc to return to the normal city camera exactly as it was. Minecraft runs hidden and
never needs focus. Spike T1 (terrain clip) is answered in game.

Contract: `protocol/minecraft-skylines-v1.md` (1.1 section), vectors in `protocol/vectors/frames.json`.
Ledger: `.agent/ledger-m2.md`.

## Design in one page

- **CS1 (host)** owns: entering/leaving player mode, OS input, look yaw/pitch, the visible camera,
  and the collision geometry it streams. Entering player mode in M2 does **not** pair or modify the
  city (no backup needed yet: nothing is written).
- **Minecraft (guest)** owns: the player's position and physics. It runs SkyCraft's `TriCollider`
  (MIT) against triangles from `COLLISION_REGION`, replays `INPUT` into its handlers as if focused,
  and publishes `PLAYER_STATE` every frame.
- **Camera:** CS1 disables its `CameraController` while in player mode, drives `Camera.main` from
  interpolated `PLAYER_STATE` (tick history, rendered ~1 tick behind on the host clock), uses MC's
  FOV and a near clip suited to first person, and restores every changed camera property on exit,
  on disconnect, on level unload and on mod disable.
- **Collision export:** terrain sampled from CS1's own height sampler on a 2 m grid (the one
  objects use, so the player stands where citizens stand), road segments as strips along their
  bezier at deck height, bridges as slabs (top and bottom faces) so you can walk under them.
  Regions of 16×16 blocks, streamed within 64 m of the player, re-sent when the player moves.
- **Reusable split:** heightfield/strip triangulation and region bookkeeping in `Skylines.Core`
  (pure C#, unit-tested on .NET 10); CS1 adapters (terrain sampler, net walker, camera, input) in
  `Skylines.Host`; Minecraft-specific glue (GLFW key map, message wiring) in `MinecraftSkylines.Mod`.
